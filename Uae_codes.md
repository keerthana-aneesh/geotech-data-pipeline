### Dewatering

- A dewatering permit from Abu Dhabi DMT is required before any dewatering activity.
- The application must include:
  - Soil investigation report
  - Dewatering method statement
  - Flow rate calculations
  - Expected settlement calculations

## Abu Dhabi Maritime Design Code

### Coastal Ground Conditions

- Aeolian sand dunes
- Coastal Sabkha / evaporite deposits extending >80 km inland
- Sedimentary bedrock: limestones, dolomites, interbedded shales and evaporites

### Key Requirements

- Sabkha cannot be used as backfill without treatment (high chloride/sulphate, collapsibility, dissolution)
- Sheet piles, gravity walls, filled caissons, reinforced earth structures must account for:
  - Sabkha deposits
  - Tidal lag
  - Liquefaction in saturated sands
  - Seismic densification
  - Dissolution cavities
